Family Kingdom

The Mix-Up Monsters took over the kingdom and scrambled every family photo. Win land for your team by asking and answering about family members.

怪獸把王國佔領了，還把家族照片全部弄亂！用英文正確問答，幫你的隊伍搶下最多領土。

Teams 隊伍

兩隊，每隊兩位同學輪流作答

Rounds 回合數

Timer 作答時間

How to play 玩法

  • 🏰Each team starts in a castle corner. Pick a land tile next to your land.
    從城堡出發，選一塊相鄰的土地挑戰。
  • ✅Teammates take turns answering. Right → the land is yours. Wrong or time's up → next team. There's plenty of time!
    隊友輪流作答。答對就佔領；答錯或時間到換下一隊。時間很充裕，慢慢想！
  • ⚔️2 right in a row → attack enemy land next to you. Right answer → it's yours.
    連對 2 題，可以挑戰旁邊的敵方土地，答對就搶過來（只搶 1 塊）。
  • 💣Bomb, choice 1: attack enemy land with a question. The bomb is used only if you answer right.
    炸彈用法一：答題搶地。答對才會用掉炸彈，答錯炸彈還在。
  • 💥Bomb, choice 2: blast any enemy tile. No question, it becomes empty land. This ends your turn.
    炸彈用法二：直接炸掉任一塊敵方土地，不用答題，變回空地。用完這回合結束。
  • 🗼2 🧱 bricks → build a tower. The tower and your land next to it get shields.
    2 塊磚可以蓋瞭望塔，塔和旁邊自己的土地都有護盾，不會被搶。
  • 🧪Potion = a hint. Every team starts with one.
    藥水可以提示，每隊一開始有 1 瓶。
  • 👑Most land at the end wins.
    最後土地最多的隊伍獲勝。
